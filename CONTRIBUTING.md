# Welcome Contributers!
Thank you for deciding to help further this project! Follow these conditions and you may be able to add your own features!

# Cleanliness of the Repository
- Make sure your code is organized and easy to understand.
- Change generic commit messages (i.e. "Update README.md -> Changed section blahblahblah in README.md)
- **Make pulls to the dev branch, not the main one!**

# Our Policy on AI
AI *can* be a useful tool but is more often than not used when someone doesn't know what they're doing.

We absolutely welcome help with this project, we love helpers! However most AI-only or primarily AI additions introduce bugs, change things that don't need to be changed, and have 0 creativity.

**We'd much rather you disclose the use of AI than leaving us to speculate.** When used as a *tool* and not the one doing all the work, we won't be as inclined to decline your contribution.
